# Message and evidence

Understand the sample and study design before trusting a headline.

Demonstrates that the pipeline exposes evidence limitations instead of converting every result into a benefit claim.

## Claims

### C1 — PDF page 2
100 men were recruited, but the intervention subgroup contained 30 men.

> Among 100 obese men, a subsample of 30 patients with MetS received personalized counseling, pamphlets, and PowerPoint presentations

Population: Men aged 36–55 in Coimbatore
Limitations: Small before-and-after intervention subgroup; no randomised comparator described. Table IV reports 8/30 as 25%, which is inconsistent. Intervention duration is unclear. Do not use effect estimates in promotional copy.

### C2 — PDF page 1
Outcomes were assessed before and after the intervention.

> The effectiveness of the program was evaluated by comparing outcomes before and after the intervention.

Population: Intervention subgroup of 30 men
Limitations: Small before-and-after intervention subgroup; no randomised comparator described. Table IV reports 8/30 as 25%, which is inconsistent. Intervention duration is unclear. Do not use effect estimates in promotional copy.

## Study limitations
- Small before-and-after intervention subgroup; no randomised comparator described. Table IV reports 8/30 as 25%, which is inconsistent. Intervention duration is unclear. Do not use effect estimates in promotional copy.
