When adults living with overweight or obesity reduce calories, diet alone can reduce both fat and fat-free mass. A 2025 systematic review of 25 randomised trials found that adding resistance exercise protected fat-free mass (moderate certainty) and increased fat mass loss (high certainty), without changing overall body weight loss. Importantly, fat-free mass is not the same as muscle alone, as it also includes body water and organs. Muscular strength was also greater (low certainty), though evidence is limited by study inconsistency. Subgroup preservation of fat-free mass was not statistically significant in trials lasting over five months. Keeping functional strength remains a valuable perspective alongside balanced meals.
