Script only; no recorded Loom is included.

**0:00–0:20:** Show PDF → evidence-linked content → doctor review → export.

**0:20–0:40:** Open extracted pages and one exact quotation, page reference, population and limitation. Quote matching does not replace clinical interpretation.

**0:40–1:05:** Show Content → Story & characters. Explain study-design story selection and topic-specific props; saved packs use local inference, new generation includes a creative brief.

**1:05–1:30:** Show carousel, static and reel. Explain illustrated animation, no narration, sound-optional viewing and evidence references. Compare the nutrition paper's plate/timeline.

**1:30–1:50:** Show review gating. All claims and final assets need current-version approval. Edits invalidate approvals and hashes protect the package.

**1:50–2:00:** Disclose clinician review pending, estimated human effort, and outstanding live validation of the newest creative schema.
